## Why from scratch

Frameworks hide exactly the parts you should understand. So I built the parts — every model implemented from first principles, no high-level ML wrappers:

- **Multi-Layer Perceptron** — MNIST digit classification, 784 → 256 → 128 → 10 with ReLU, Adam, He init, L2 regularization. 97%+ test accuracy. [Live demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Eli181927/elliot_digit_classifier/)
- **Convolutional Neural Network** — two-digit classification (0–99). 97.88% accuracy. [Live demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Eli181927/0-99_Classification)
- **Recurrent Neural Network** — doodle classification across 10 animals. 94.36% accuracy. [Live demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Eli181927/Classification-doodle-RNN)
- **Transformers** — "Attention Is All You Need" re-implemented end-to-end: an encoder for emotion analysis, a decoder trained as a Shakespeare generator, and the full encoder–decoder translating English to French. [Live demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Eli181927/Transformer_Demo)

## What I learned

Backprop stops being magic the third time you derive it. The payoff shows up everywhere else: when a training run misbehaves in PyTorch, I have a mental model of what's actually happening in the graph — because I've built the graph.

The fundamentals repo is [here](https://github.com/Elliot-Sones/Neural_Networks_Fundamentals) and the transformer deep-dive (encoder/decoder walkthroughs) is [here](https://github.com/Elliot-Sones/Transformers).
